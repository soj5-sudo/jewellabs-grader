How it works

How a stone becomes a filing.

  1. 01 Cut plan A rough scan goes to the model. It sends back where to cut.
  2. 02 Read The stone goes in the box and is graded in seconds.
  3. 03 Record One row per stone, readable by any other system.
  4. 04 File Checked against the certificate and invoice, then filed.

One record, shared by every machine.
