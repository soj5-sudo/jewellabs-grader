03

How it works.

  1. 01LoadSealed chamber. No outside light, which is why the numbers repeat.
  2. 02LightCalibrated arrays, plus a 365 nm pass for fluorescence.
  3. 03MeasureStructured light maps the facets. A load cell takes the weight.
  4. 04SeparateA spectrometer separates natural from CVD and HPHT.
  5. 05GradeThe model assigns every category that carries price, with a confidence.
  6. 06FileA second model turns those records into the customs filing.
