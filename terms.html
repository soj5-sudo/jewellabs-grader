How to read the grading claims

The system assigns grades by machine, from measured optical, spectral and dimensional signals. It is an instrument, not a gemmological laboratory, and its output is not a laboratory report. Where value or a filing turns on a grade, treat the record as a measurement and apply your own commercial judgement.

Where the system identifies a stone as laboratory grown, it records it as laboratory grown, in that wording. Disclosure at the point of sale remains the seller’s obligation under the FTC Jewelry Guides at 16 CFR Part 23.

The documents shown on this site
